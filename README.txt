CAMPUSNEST STUDENT HOSTEL WEBSITE
=================================

Pages included (7):
1. index.html       - Home
2. about.html       - About us + uploaded hostel manager photograph
3. facilities.html  - Hostel facilities
4. food.html        - Home-style food and sample daily menu
5. cleanliness.html- Neatness and cleaning routine
6. rooms.html       - Single / two / three sharing rooms and sample fees
7. contact.html     - Contact information and enquiry form

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract CampusNest_Hostel_Website.zip.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in Chrome/Edge.
4. Internet is required for Google Fonts and the remote sample photos.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMISATION
- Replace sample phone number, email, address, room fees and meal timings with real details.
- The uploaded image is placed in assets/hostel-manager.jpeg and displayed on About Us.
- Update the manager's name in about.html when you provide the correct name.
- The enquiry form opens the visitor's email app; it does not store submissions online.
  Connect a backend or form service for real online booking/enquiry storage.
- The website is a front-end demo. It does not yet include a database, login,
  payment gateway, live room availability or admin dashboard.
